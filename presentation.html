The 4 ML Models

Four different forecasting approaches are compared to find the most accurate prediction for each product.

S

SMA Baseline

Baseline

What it captures: The simplest model — a 7-day average of recent sales. It captures the general level of demand but ignores trends, seasonality, and all other patterns.

Role: Serves as the benchmark. Every other model must beat this baseline to justify its complexity. If a sophisticated model cannot outperform SMA, it is not adding value.

Config: Simple 7-day average. No training required.

H

Holt-Winters

Statistical

What it captures: Three components of time series — the baseline level, the direction of change (trend), and repeating cycles (seasonality). Specifically designed for data with regular patterns.

Role: The go-to model when you expect regular patterns like weekly cycles. It automatically adapts to changes in demand over time.

Config: Tuned for weekly seasonal patterns with automatic parameter optimization.

A

ARIMA

Statistical

What it captures: How past values influence future values, removes trends to find stable patterns, and corrects for one-time events. Handles changing data gracefully.

Role: Best for data where past patterns strongly influence future values. Automatically selects the optimal settings, removing the need for manual tuning.

Config: Automatically selects optimal parameters for daily data. No manual tuning needed.

X

XGBoost

Machine Learning

What it captures: Complex, non-linear patterns that other models miss. Learns relationships between multiple factors like recent sales, time of week, and seasonal trends.

Role: The most flexible model. Best when demand is influenced by many factors interacting in complex ways.

Config: Uses recent sales history, weekly trends, and calendar effects as inputs. Automatically learns complex patterns.

Model Comparison Results

All models evaluated on the same held-out test set using standard forecasting metrics.

ModelRMSEMAEBiasImprovement over SMA

Key Takeaway: Model Progression

Each successive model adds sophistication and captures more complex patterns:

  • SMA (71.15): Simple average — no pattern learning at all
  • Holt-Winters (66.03): Adds trend + seasonality — 7.2% better
  • ARIMA (63.51): Adds autoregressive + error correction — 10.7% best
  • XGBoost (64.92): Adds non-linear features — 8.8% with lowest bias

Metric Definitions

  • RMSE: Overall forecast error (penalizes large misses). Lower is better.
  • MAE: Average error per prediction. Lower is better.
  • Bias: Tendency to over or under forecast. Closer to zero is better.

Inventory Optimization

Once we know what demand will be, the system calculates the optimal order quantities and timing to minimize costs.

Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)

Optimal order size that minimizes total ordering + holding costs.

The system calculates the ideal order size by balancing the cost of placing orders against the cost of storing inventory.

Automated Recommendations

The system generates prioritized, actionable recommendations based on forecast output and current inventory state.

CRITICAL

Critical Restock

Product will hit zero stock within 24–48 hours. Demand forecast exceeds current stock + pipeline orders. Immediate supplier contact required.

HIGH

High Priority Restock

Product will fall below safety stock within 3–5 days. Order should be placed this week to arrive before threshold breach.

TRANSFER

Inter-Store Transfer

One outlet is overstocked while another is running low on the same product. A transfer is more cost-effective than ordering new stock.

OVERSTOCK

Overstock Warning

Current stock exceeds 30-day forecasted demand. Consider markdowns, promotions, or halting reorders to avoid expiration and waste.

How Recommendations Are Generated

The system automatically evaluates every product at every outlet and assigns a priority level:

  • Calculates how many days of stock remain
  • Compares current stock against the forecast
  • Factors in supplier lead times
  • Checks if another outlet has excess to transfer
  • Assigns a priority level (Critical to Monitor)
  • Recommends specific quantities and actions
